Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026 (sign-in now uses a normal Google page instead of a pop-up window)

In short

  • remini runs entirely in your browser, on your phone or computer.
  • It reads your Google Drive (read-only) only to show you your own photos and videos.
  • Nothing is sent to any server other than Google.
  • There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking. There are no cookies, apart from the ones Google uses for signing in.

What remini reads

When you sign in with Google, you give remini permission to read files in your Google Drive. remini uses this only to find your photo list (a file called remini-catalog.json) and to show the photos, small previews and videos it lists.

remini can't change, move, share or delete anything in your Google Drive.

What stays on your device

To open quickly, remini keeps your photo list and small previews of your photos in your browser's storage on this device. They never leave the device. Full-size photos are not kept.

When you sign in, Google gives remini a pass (an "access token") that lets it read your Drive for about an hour. remini keeps this pass only for the current browser session: it is cleared when you close the tab or the app, and it stops working within an hour anyway. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.

remini also remembers which Google account you used last on this device, so Google can suggest it the next time you sign in.

To remove everything remini saved, open Accounts and tap Sign out and forget this phone. Clearing your browser's data for this site does the same.

Who else sees your photos

No one. The photos go directly from Google to your browser. remini has no server of its own, and the people who made it can't see your photos or your photo list.

Taking back permission

You can take back remini's permission at any time in your Google Account, under Security, then "Your connections to third-party apps and services".
