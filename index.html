What is remini?

remini shows your own photos and videos, kept safe in your Google Drive, on your phone, even when your computer is off.

There are no "memories", no ranking and no feeds. Just your photos, newest first, with a timeline and a search. You decide how things are grouped.

Sign in

Sign in with your home Google account to see your photos. remini only reads; it can't change or delete anything.

Your photos go straight from Google to this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
